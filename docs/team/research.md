# Ternary Cargo: market and regulatory research

*Desk research only, 31 October 2026. No one was contacted. Every claim has a source URL. Builds on TS-RR-2026-01 (https://docs.google.com/document/d/1siM_Nf_cCKPL5_W0U81Hz4V09HUbf5BAvtf07QPjoUE/edit) and TS-RR-2026-02 (https://docs.google.com/document/d/1HHlAnzB4H7gl9HNbXLI3ScfSb07glwbnfGZ2RplISxo/edit). **GAP** marks missing data.*

- Merchandise exports were US$13,579.38 mn in 2025 (+6.32%). Total goods and services exports were US$17,252.15 mn (EDB press release), or US$17,319 mn in the EDB indicators table. Source: https://www.ft.lk/top-story/Export-earnings-up-5-6-to-17-2-b-in-2025/26-787471 , https://www.srilankabusiness.com/ebooks/export-performance-indicators-of-sri-lanka-2025.pdf

- The Port of Colombo handled 8,291,178 TEU in 2025, up 6.4%. Source: https://news.slpa.lk/index.php/2026/01/01/port-of-colombo-records-historic-high-of-8-29-million-teus-in-2025/
- BIA handled 193,873 t of cargo in 2025 (AASL) and 192,498 t in 2024 (SLFFA country report). Source: https://www.airport.lk/aasl , https://www.fapaa.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Country-Report-2025-SLFFA.pdf

## 4. Top 5 pain points

1. **Fragmented systems mean re-keying.** Data goes separately into ASYCUDA, ASYHUB, SLPA's two cargo systems, SriLankan Cargo, DoC e-CoO, chamber eCO and agency portals. Evidence: the systems and logins listed in §3. Customs ranked 84th of 139 on the LPI 2023 customs sub-index (https://www.ft.lk/front-page/Sri-Lanka-improves-in-latest-global-Logistics-Performance-Index/44-747682). Customs caused 40.79% of domestic procedural obstacles for exporters (ITC, 2011, dated: https://www.sasec.asia/uploads/events/2014/tfweek-2014/am/itc-ntm-report-sri-lanka.pdf). **GAP:** hours lost to re-keying.
2. **Many agencies per product, with stale guidance.** A desiccated coconut exporter needs CDA registration, an MoH health certificate, a CoO and a CusDec. The EDB guide's CDA fee (Rs 10,000) differs from CDA's own 2026 fee (Rs 15,000). Sources: https://www.srilankabusiness.com/pdf/export-procedure-english.pdf , https://www.cda.gov.lk/web/images/pdf/2026_REGISTRATION/2026_Guidelines.pdf , https://eohfs.health.gov.lk/food/index.php?Itemid=140&id=6&lang=en&option=com_content&view=article
3. **Congestion and clearance shocks.** The January 2025 Colombo backlog brought demurrage charges and skipped calls (https://www.sundaytimes.lk/250119/news/multiple-factors-combine-to-clog-cargo-in-colombos-port-585097.html). In July 2025, about 40 t of parcels piled up after a duty-method change (https://ceylontoday.lk/2025/07/09/as-new-tax-method-takes-effect-40-mt-of-parcels-pile-up-at-warehouses/). **GAP:** current dwell-time data, since there is no time-release study.
4. **Little visibility after hand-over.** Manifests in ASYHUB are filed by carriers and forwarders, not exporters (https://www.customs.gov.lk/services/asyhub/). Tracking is split across carriers (https://www.srilankancargo.com/products/product-categories). **GAP:** no source quantifies how much quoting and tracking runs on email and WhatsApp (TS-RR-2026-01 §3.2).
5. **High small-shipment and payment costs.** DHL charges US$45.87–70.04 for 1 kg before surcharges (https://mydhl.express.dhl/content/dam/downloads/lk/en/rate-guide/service_and_rate_guide_lk_en_2025.pdf.coredownload.pdf). No public LCL rates were found. Export proceeds must be repatriated within 180 days, and what is left must be converted by the 10th of the following month (https://www.dfe.lk/web/index.php?Itemid=729&id=391&lang=en&option=com_content&view=article). Stripe does not support Sri Lanka (https://stripe.com/global). PayPal is limited to personal accounts (https://www.paypal.com/lk/business/banking-partners).
